### Copyright and licensing

- No image was copied from the websites, Michelin Guide, Tripadvisor, Instagram, Google Maps or any stock-photo provider. Therefore, the bundle does not depend on third-party stock licenses or copyrighted venue photographs.
- AI-generated output does not necessarily receive copyright protection in every jurisdiction; do **not** claim universal copyright ownership or exclusivity. The package contains no copied third-party photograph, but no absolute guarantee against resemblance or conflicting rights is possible.
- Preserve the editorial disclosure directly below the image and in the editorial-methodology note. A link or photographer credit alone would NOT authorize reuse of an otherwise copyrighted restaurant photo.
- If replacing an illustration with a genuine venue image later, obtain a license or permission appropriate for commercial editorial web use, record the author, source URL, license/version or written permission, allowed adaptations, attribution and any expiry, and update visible credits/alt/schema/OG tags.
- Sources describing dishes are **reference links only**, not image-use permissions.

### If TMR requires documentary restaurant photography

Replace these conceptual images before public launch with photos licensed directly by the restaurant/photographer or sourced from verified free-to-use CC0/CC BY assets (observe exact license requirements). Do not use photos from Tripadvisor, Google, Michelin or Instagram merely because they are public. Do not silently re-label an unrelated dish photo as food cooked at the featured restaurant.
