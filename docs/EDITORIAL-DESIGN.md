# Editorial design direction — The Mexico Review
Updated: 2026-10-13

## Visual language
- Quiet, editorial and restrained: warm paper, wine red, dark green accents, generous whitespace, refined serif typography.
- Photographs carry the visual weight, not gratuitous UI decoration.
- Avoid hover-jump cards, large arrow glyphs, fake medallions, repeated decorative lines and generic gradients.
- Keep image captions and credits discreet but available; do not suggest photographed venues were reviewed without evidence.
- Use verified authentic photographs from the actual destinations, with subject-specific alternate text and stable image URLs. Image licenses must be checked.
- Editorial pages remain noindex while placeholders. This is not a promise of any review or winner.
- Format navigation: Reviews, Best Of, Annual Awards (first considered edition planned for year-end 2026, conditional on defensible coverage).
- Destinations: Cancún, Mexico City, Los Cabos. Categories: Restaurants, Hotels, Experiences.
- Responsive photo layouts: clear mobile reading order, no hidden content or hover-only links.
