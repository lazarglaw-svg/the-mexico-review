# Phase: City guides and category directories (October 2026)

## Published structure
- 3 detailed destination city guides in es-MX and en-US: `/cancun/`, `/mexico-city/`, `/los-cabos/` (plus `/en/` counterparts).
- Each guide has editorial introduction, verified geographic/cultural facts, 3 key neighbourhood/area highlights, 2 region-specific gallery images, links to Restaurants, Hotels, Experiences, and collapsible factual source references.
- City guides are indexable and included in sitemap with reciprocal hreflang entries.
- 3 category directories (`/restaurants/`, `/hotels/`, `/experiences/`) plus English counterparts.
- 9 city x category landing pages plus English counterparts.
- Category directory dropdown filter navigates to city-category pages. City-category pages can switch back to all destinations.
- No invented reviewed establishments, popularity rankings, scores or awards. Category and city-category pages remain `noindex,follow` until real content is available.

## City fact sources
- Cancún: INAH Museo Maya / El Rey and Nichupté, with authentic Cancún images by Willian Justen de Vasconcellos and Héctor Mavare.
- Ciudad de México: INEGI area 1,494.3 km²; 16 alcaldías; approx. elevation 2,240 metres. City tourism guide for Roma / Condesa. Photography by María Fuentes and Jesus Mora.
- Los Cabos: Visit Los Cabos tourism board — Cabo San Lucas, San José del Cabo and roughly 32-km Tourist Corridor. Photography by Andrea Garibay and Marco Samaniego.
- All city photo pages are on Unsplash and have separate photographer/source notes. They are representative location images, not evidence of reviewed establishments.

## Rules for next stages
- Continue parallel localization: root Spanish, /en English.
- Build specific category reviews later once real content and evidence have been supplied.
- Avoid publishing "Best Restaurants" rankings when no reviewed restaurants exist.
- City filter currently performs direct page navigation; no database, CMS or dynamically queried review data.
