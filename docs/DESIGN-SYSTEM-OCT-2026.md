# Editorial UI design system — October 2026

Brand intent: restrained luxury, clearly delineated editorial sections, no ornamental gradients or glossy shadows. Bourgogne `#4B0F17`, green `#183C32`, stone/off-white surfaces.

## Homepage
- Hero now uses a genuine Riviera Maya architectural photograph by Dayana Brooke, licensed under Unsplash License (https://unsplash.com/license); original https://unsplash.com/photos/two-red-and-beige-clay-pots-Fdu-sAZ32dk. This photograph appears only in the homepage hero, in Spanish and English as corresponding localized versions.
- Hero is a cream editorial feature with a narrow framing border, distinct edition label, generous typography, understated exploration CTA, photo credit directly on image. Home OG image mirrors hero.
- Editorial principles section uses a separate Palacio Postal Mexico City architectural photograph by Ricardo Loaiza (https://unsplash.com/photos/a-staircase-in-a-building-with-a-skylight-above-it-wiNLYz5c4wE); credit under photo. This removes the former repeated Bellas Artes image from home.
- Existing destination preview images are deliberately reused only as identity-matching images within their own destination pages or destination directory navigation. Article preview images match their respective stories.

## Site-wide
- Section headings have bottom delineation; introductory explanatory blurbs have subtle left rules.
- Destination and category grids are framed warm-paper cards, no shadows and no radius.
- Editorial cards, restaurant guides, Journal cards and Awards blocks receive analogous understated boundaries.
- Alternating warm surfaces and 1px neutral / Bordeaux-tinted borders identify section changes.
- City facts, neighborhoods, category links, city selector entries, restaurant cards, hotel Journal and Awards form use consistent surface palette.
- Keep actual editorial photography, credit links, unique alt text, and avoid repeating unrelated hero imagery.
- Responsive breakpoints down to 670px ensure cards stack, full-width hero images appear after copy, and fact cards become vertical.
- No changes to URL topology, SEO canonicals or schema claims.

For new sections: start with a subtle uppercase overline, generous serif heading, intro line on a clear divider, content on a single defined surface; avoid red/green filled callout boxes, ornaments and faux award graphics.
