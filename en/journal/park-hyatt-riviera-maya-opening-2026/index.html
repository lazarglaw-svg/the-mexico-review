HOTEL OPENINGS

Park Hyatt Riviera Maya: Inside the First All-Inclusive Park Hyatt

A planned 148-room coastal resort near Cancún aims to combine Park Hyatt’s quiet design language with a more ambitious all-inclusive model.

By The Mexico Review·October 16, 2026

Can an all-inclusive hotel feel intimate?

All-inclusive resorts usually promise convenience: meals arranged, drinks covered and fewer decisions once the holiday begins. Park Hyatt has traditionally promised something different—considered design, individual attention and hospitality at a less hurried pace. The brand’s new Riviera Maya project aims to bring these ideas together.

Planned as the world’s first all-inclusive Park Hyatt, the resort could become an interesting test of whether an inclusive package can still feel personal. Its proposed restaurant programme and relatively restrained room count point to a different approach from large-scale all-inclusive hotels.

Opening date: what is actually confirmed?

Hyatt announced on September 29, 2026 that Park Hyatt Riviera Maya was expected to open by the end of 2026. The hotel’s official website already presents rooms and booking options, but that should not be mistaken for confirmation the hotel is operating.

Earlier travel reporting indicated reservable stays from February 2027. As project timelines and booking calendars can change, confirm the first actual check-in date directly with Hyatt before committing to flights or non-refundable plans. As of this October 2026 report, we describe it as an upcoming opening.

Where is Park Hyatt Riviera Maya?

The site sits within the AMAI Coastal Enclave along Federal Highway 307 at kilometre 333, between Cancún and Puerto Morelos. Hyatt describes a coastal location bordered by natural vegetation, with the airport roughly 20 minutes away in suitable traffic conditions.

Rather than a hotel within the walkable parts of Cancún’s Hotel Zone, this is intended as a self-contained Riviera Maya retreat. Our Cancún destination guide offers more context for exploring beyond the resort.

Rooms, suites and design

Hyatt advertises 148 rooms and suites with private balconies and a contemporary design influenced by natural materials and Mexican craftsmanship. Some categories include private pools or plunge pools, with larger suites and penthouse accommodation also listed.

The exact differences matter: a pool may be private or shared depending on room category, and ocean views are not interchangeable across every type. Examine the official room categories before booking. Pre-opening visualisations cannot establish how a finished hotel will actually feel.

Seven places to eat and drink

Food is central to the proposition. Hyatt describes seven restaurants and bars, with concepts including Bruma steakhouse, IK’AL beach grill, Nutre Kitchen, Patisserie Market & Café, the Brisa pool bar and Living Room lounge. Brand announcements have also referenced The Botanist cocktail concept.

There is one detail worth clarifying. A Hyatt press release calls the offering “entirely à la carte,” but the hotel’s dining page specifically describes four à la carte venues and buffet-style breakfast and cooking stations at Nutre Kitchen. It is more accurate to expect a combination of à la carte restaurants and all-day dining, rather than promise every meal is exclusively à la carte.

What is included in the package?

Hyatt’s published list includes meals, selected drinks, a replenished minibar, 24-hour in-room dining, concierge or Resort Ambassador support, pool access, fitness facilities and non-motorised watersports. Hydrotherapy access at Velara Spa & Wellness is also described, while individual treatments, excursions and premium services may cost extra.

For eligible direct reservations of four nights or more, the hotel advertises a private airport transfer subject to terms. Check the details of the specific rate, particularly premium drinks, spa treatments, transport and cancellation conditions.

Spa, coastline and pools

Plans feature four swimming pools, including an adults-only pool, plus a beachfront setting and the Velara Spa & Wellness concept. Hyatt has described approximately 300 metres of beachfront and an emphasis on hydrotherapy and restorative treatments.

An adults-only pool does not imply the entire hotel is adults-only. Couples and honeymooners may find the positioning appealing, but family policies must be checked directly rather than assumed.

Should you book?

The proposition will likely appeal most to travellers who want the convenience of an inclusive stay but also care about design, food and attentive service. Guests hoping for an already-established operating track record may prefer to compare other Riviera Maya and Cancún properties while this hotel approaches opening.

For context, our guide to Mexico’s new MICHELIN Key hotels explores other hospitality styles, from full-scale coastal resorts to boutique escapes. Avoid treating a single advertised nightly price as definitive: rates vary by date, room category and inclusions. What matters is the total cost of the stay you intend to have.

Frequently asked questions

When will Park Hyatt Riviera Maya open?

Hyatt’s September 2026 announcement expected the resort to open by year-end 2026. Confirm the actual first check-in date with the hotel.

Is Park Hyatt Riviera Maya all-inclusive?

Yes. Hyatt says it will be the brand’s first all-inclusive Park Hyatt, with dining and several services included subject to rate terms.

How many rooms does it have?

The hotel advertises 148 rooms and suites, including select categories with private pools or plunge pools.

Where is it?

At Federal Highway 307 kilometre 333 in the AMAI Coastal Enclave, between Cancún and Puerto Morelos.

Is the whole resort adults-only?

No whole-property adults-only policy has been confirmed. An adults-only pool is not the same as an adults-only hotel.

What are the dining options?

Hyatt describes seven restaurants and bars, including Bruma, IK’AL, Nutre Kitchen, Patisserie Market & Café, Brisa and Living Room.

Where can I check current rates?

Use the official Hyatt booking site with your actual dates, guest count and room category, and check rate-specific inclusions.
