Casa Tortugas, Cancún

A sunset-facing lagoon pool, suites with kitchen facilities and a family-run atmosphere offer another side of Cancún’s Hotel Zone.

Hotel Guide · Updated October 22, 2026

Lagoon-side poolOpen views across the Nichupté Lagoon.
Practical suitesKitchen-equipped rooms for independent stays.
Playa TortugasBeach and Isla Mujeres ferry nearby.

A quieter pocket of the Hotel Zone

Cancún’s Hotel Zone is often associated with all-inclusive towers. Casa Tortugas, on Calle Cenzontle near kilometre 6.5 of Boulevard Kukulcán, offers a smaller and more independent approach. It faces the Nichupté Lagoon, near Playa Tortugas and the ferry connections toward Isla Mujeres.

The waterfront here is a lagoon, not the Caribbean Sea: Casa Tortugas does not have a private beachfront immediately in front of it. Guests can reach the nearby beach across the main road. Swimming in the lagoon is prohibited because crocodiles may be present.

Suites built around flexibility

The booking collection includes Studio, Junior, Master and Two-Bedroom suites. Apartment-style amenities and kitchen facilities allow guests to prepare their own meals and organise their days without resort schedules.

This setup is particularly helpful for small families or longer visits, but room configurations and equipment depend on the category you reserve. Casa Tortugas is not designed around an all-inclusive food-and-drink package.

The view, the pool and the people

The lagoon-facing pool is central to the atmosphere. Open sunset views and a small-hotel feel make an appealing contrast to activity-packed resort complexes.

Guests frequently highlight helpful hosts and clean accommodation. The supplied October 2026 Booking.com snapshot showed 9.0/10 from 787 guest reviews, with staff at 9.5, comfort at 9.4 and cleanliness at 9.3. These figures are platform scores, not a rating awarded by The Mexico Review.

The house rules that matter

Casa Tortugas publishes detailed policies. Check-in begins at 3 p.m., and check-out is at 11 a.m. In the supplied policy snapshot, arrivals after 9 p.m. carried a US$29 staffing charge. The primary virtual reception operates through WhatsApp during set hours.

External visitors are not allowed, parking is limited, housekeeping for longer stays is listed as every fourth day, and the pool’s published hours are 8 a.m. to 8 p.m. Quiet hours start after 10 p.m. Terms can change, so read the official house rules before booking.

Who will enjoy Casa Tortugas?

The strengths are a useful Hotel Zone location, relaxed lagoon outlook, practical suites and the freedom to explore the city. It is a natural fit for travellers who would rather organise their own meals and day trips.

For a sense of the wider area, read our Cancún destination guide. Those seeking a resort with private Caribbean beach access, unlimited food and entertainment will be happier considering another category of property.
