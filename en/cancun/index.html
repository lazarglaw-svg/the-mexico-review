Cancún, Quintana Roo
Getting to know the destination

More than a beach destination.

Cancún lies in northern Quintana Roo on Mexico’s Caribbean coast. Its Hotel Zone follows Kukulcán Boulevard between beaches, hotels, restaurants and Nichupté Lagoon. It is the destination’s best-known image, but only one part of the story.

Downtown Cancún has markets, neighbourhood restaurants and the daily life of a working city. Farther north, Puerto Juárez is a gateway to Isla Mujeres. Cancún also has a Maya heritage: the Museo Maya, San Miguelito and the El Rey archaeological site offer a view beyond its modern tourism infrastructure.
