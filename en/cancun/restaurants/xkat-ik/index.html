Xkat-Ik: Yucatecan Cooking by the Lagoon

Traditional flavours, bright citrus and a lagoon-side setting in the heart of Cancún's Hotel Zone.

The Mexico Review · Restaurant Guide · October 2026

A taste of the Yucatán in Cancún

Between the resort dining rooms of the Hotel Zone and the quiet water of Nichupté Lagoon, Xkat-Ik offers something distinctly rooted in the peninsula: a menu built around Yucatecan flavours, citrus marinades, achiote and familiar regional preparations.

You'll find it along Boulevard Kukulcán at kilometre 16.2, close to the Museo Maya. Its location makes it an accessible stop for travellers who want to explore the food of the region without leaving the Hotel Zone. The lagoon-facing garden adds another dimension, particularly around sunset.

The dishes to know

The menu brings together Yucatecan classics and contemporary Mexican options. These are some of the preparations that best express its regional character; dishes and availability can change.

Cochinita pibil

Achiote, citrus and slow-cooked pork are central to one of the Yucatán's signature dishes. Wrapped in banana leaves as it cooks, cochinita pibil is a natural starting point for anyone new to the region's cuisine. Pickled red onion brings the sharp contrast associated with the preparation.

Chicken in the style of poc chuc

Sour orange gives this grilled chicken preparation its unmistakable Yucatecan direction. Beans, pickled onion and guacamole complete a plate that focuses on citrus, the grill and uncomplicated regional flavours. The traditional pork-based poc chuc provides its inspiration.

Molcajete Valladolid

For something heartier, the Molcajete Valladolid combines chicken, pork, Valladolid-style sausage, panela cheese, nopales and grilled spring onion. Recado rojo, salsa ranchera and guacamole make it a generous introduction to several flavours of the peninsula in one dish.

Xkatik vegetariano

The restaurant's namesake xkat-ik pepper also appears as a vegetarian preparation, with a vegetable or cheese filling, potato purée and tomato sauce. It's a useful alternative for diners seeking a meal centred on a regional ingredient rather than meat.

A different side of the Hotel Zone

Part of Xkat-Ik's appeal is the contrast between its address and its atmosphere. Although the restaurant sits on the Hotel Zone's main boulevard, the garden and lagoon create a more relaxed backdrop for an evening meal. It is well suited to an unhurried dinner, especially for travellers interested in food that speaks to the wider Yucatán Peninsula.

The setting is informal rather than tasting-menu formal. If a table near the water is important to you, it's worth asking when you reserve. Parking can be limited, so arriving by taxi is another option.

Plan your visit

Where: Boulevard Kukulcán, Km 16.2, Zona Hotelera, Cancún, near the Museo Maya.

Contact: +52 998 885 3538. For opening hours, reservations and the latest dishes, visit the Xkat-Ik website.

Why put Xkat-Ik on your list?

Cancún offers countless places to eat, but relatively few combine a convenient Hotel Zone address, Yucatecan-inspired cooking and a lagoon-side garden. For anyone curious about cochinita pibil, sour orange and the region's distinctive peppers, Xkat-Ik is a name worth knowing.
