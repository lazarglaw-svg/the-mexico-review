Nima Local House, Mexico City

Four rooms, a historic Colima Street address and an unusually personal approach to hospitality give this Roma Norte hideaway the feel of a quietly elegant residence.

Hotel Guide · Updated October 22, 2026

Four roomsAn unusually private scale in a capital city.

The house, not a hotel tower

Behind a discreet doorway at Colima 236, Nima occupies a restored Roma Norte residence. Its period architecture, contemporary furniture and Mexican detailing make it markedly different from a large city hotel. The appeal is an intimate, residential atmosphere rather than a sprawling list of facilities.

With only four rooms, there are no long corridors of identical accommodation. The setting suits travellers who want to explore the neighbourhood and return to somewhere quiet and personal.

Four rooms, each with a distinct personality

The current room collection comprises Executive Lebreton, Deluxe De la Lama, Suite Tovar y de Teresa and Executive Loaeza. All four are advertised with king-size beds. Balcony positions, proportions and views differ, giving each a character of its own.

Lebreton and Loaeza feature interior balconies; De la Lama looks toward the garden dome, while the Tovar y de Teresa suite is presented as a more art-and-history-oriented choice. Breakfast is listed as included with the hotel’s room categories. Always check the precise configuration when reserving.

Breakfast, spa and Roma Norte beyond the door

Inside the house, Solarium Nima provides an intimate setting for breakfast and light dining. The property also promotes Nima Urban Spa, with wellness treatments and massages offered separately.

The neighbourhood itself is part of the reason to choose Nima. Around Roma Norte are independent coffee shops, galleries, boutiques and an unusually varied dining scene. You can explore more in our Mexico City guide or pair your stay with the city’s notable cocktail bars.

What guest feedback suggests

In the supplied October 2026 research snapshot, Booking.com displayed 9.8/10 from 67 guest reviews. That is a relatively small sample, but the repeated praise for staff, bedding and location is consistent with editorial accounts of Nima’s personalised approach.

Guest scores belong to their review platforms, not The Mexico Review. They can change, and we do not present them as our own hotel rating.

Before you book

Availability may be limited because there are only four rooms. The premium price is better understood as paying for privacy, design and attentiveness than for a large resort-style pool or gym.

Historic room layouts and access may not suit every mobility need, and Expedia’s listing indicates no on-site parking. Confirm access, room selection, spa treatments and breakfast inclusions directly with the property.

Who is it for?
