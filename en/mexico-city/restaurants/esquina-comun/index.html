Esquina Común: A Creative Corner of Condesa

An intimate, leafy rooftop where chef Ana Dolores González puts an inventive spin on contemporary Mexican cooking.

The Mexico Review · Restaurant Guide · October 2026

An unexpected dining room above Condesa

Not every Michelin-starred restaurant announces itself with a grand entrance. Esquina Común takes a different approach: a compact, greenery-filled rooftop in Condesa, with a relaxed atmosphere and cooking that puts creativity ahead of ceremony.

Chef Ana Dolores González leads a kitchen whose dishes move between recognisable Mexican ingredients and less expected combinations. What began as a small project in Roma in 2021 has evolved into one of Condesa's notable contemporary dining addresses.

The restaurant holds one Michelin star in the 2026 Michelin Guide Mexico, a distinction that reflects its culinary ambition without changing the intimate scale of the space. The format leans toward a tasting-style progression, with several dishes designed to share.

Mexican ingredients, different possibilities

Masa, chiles, fish, fruit and familiar sauces provide the foundation here. The kitchen's interest lies in how those elements can be reimagined. Because the menu evolves, the dishes below offer a sense of its direction rather than a fixed checklist for dinner.

Fish with mole verde

A fish preparation with green mole, plantain, fava beans and pumpkin seeds shows the way Esquina Común works across savoury depth, sweetness and traditional Mexican ingredients. It is an example of how the kitchen brings a familiar sauce into a contemporary setting.

Fish tamal and salsa macha

Another combination pairs fish with masa, plantain and the chile-rich character of salsa macha. The tamal format feels rooted in Mexican cooking, while the seafood filling makes room for a different expression.

Tuna tostada

Crisp corn, tuna, potato and chile güero come together in a version of the tostada that captures the restaurant's playful side. It is a useful window into the kitchen's interest in texture and contrast.

Barbacoa sope and seafood crudo

The range extends from a pork barbacoa sope with beans, pasilla chile and avocado to seafood crudo accented by tropical fruit and citrus notes. Traditional preparations and freer interpretations coexist here rather than competing for attention.

Small in scale, ambitious in spirit

Esquina Común is not the place to look for an oversized dining room or a menu that never changes. Its identity rests on a smaller space, an expressive kitchen and an approach that allows familiar ingredients to take on unexpected forms.

The leafy terrace and sharing-friendly format make it an appealing name for travellers exploring Mexico City's more inventive restaurant scene. With limited seating and a Michelin star, planning ahead is sensible.

Planning a table

Where: Fernando Montes de Oca 86, Condesa, Cuauhtémoc, Ciudad de México.

Reservations: Check Esquina Común's Instagram for announcements and current contact details, or consult its OpenTable page.

Menus may vary with the kitchen's changing ideas. If a particular preparation is important to your plans, it's worth asking ahead of time.

Why Esquina Común belongs on the radar

In a city filled with ambitious places to eat, Esquina Común has a distinctive proposition: inventive Mexican food in an intimate Condesa setting. For diners drawn to masa, seafood, chiles and a more relaxed kind of Michelin-starred dining, it offers plenty to be curious about.
