The Mexico Review Standard

A recommendation should be earned.

Popularity is not the same as quality. The Mexico Review is being built around transparent editorial judgment, first-hand evidence where a review makes experiential claims, and a clear separation between editorial recognition and commercial relationships.

01
Independent judgmentPaid placement never determines a score, ranking or award.
02
Evidence before verdictReviews are based on documented visits and verifiable details, not invented first-hand experience.
03
Useful specificityPrices, strengths, trade-offs and practical context matter more than generic praise.
04
Living recommendationsImportant guides and collections are revisited as places, prices and standards change.
