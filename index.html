The Mexico Review standard

Good taste begins with independent judgement.

We aim to be selective, specific and useful. Personal experience is identified as such; research is supported by sources. No placement or editorial award can be purchased.

Our first destination is Cancún. Reviews and recommendations will be published only as the supporting work is completed.
